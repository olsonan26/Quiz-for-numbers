# Visualization System

The full profile keeps every required view with plain headings:

1. Your Main Patterns
2. How You Change Under Stress
3. What Motivates You
4. How You Communicate
5. Conflict Process Map
6. What You Need and Notice
7. How Your Patterns Work Together
8. How Name and Number Ideas Compare
9. How Certain These Results Are
10. What to Try First
11. Settings That May Fit You
12. A Shareable Guide

Decision-making also has a compact Decision Style view.

## Disclosure order

The report shows the goal-specific direct answer, three recommendation cards, and only the two or three goal-relevant visuals first. All remaining charts begin inside a closed section titled **Explore your full profile**. The optional founder-source number/name layer and version/evidence details appear after the practical results.

The user can understand the main answer without opening any graph. Charts support the answer instead of serving as the answer.

## Accessibility

Each chart states the question it answers and has a structured data-table alternative. What You Need and Notice uses visible band words and horizontal bars; its meaning is not represented by unexplained dots or color alone. Playwright verifies 320px containment and captures desktop/mobile fixtures under `docs/assessment/screenshots/`.
