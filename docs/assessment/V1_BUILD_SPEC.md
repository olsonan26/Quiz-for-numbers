# V1 Build Specification

## Locked product

Human Pattern Profile is a local-first, developmental assessment for understanding a person through behavioral evidence, context, stress shifts, and a separately identified proprietary name/number hypothesis layer.

- Users: adults completing self, child-observer, partner-observer, or other-observer paths.
- Goals: communication, motivation, emotional reactions, conflict, decisions, stress, strengths, direction/fit, or overall understanding.
- Constructs: HUE-01 through HUE-12 exactly as defined in the governing construct map.
- Candidate bank: 72 original items; 36 minimum, 42 typical, 48 maximum.
- Completion target: 8–12 minutes in real pilot use.
- Reports: all 15 result modules and all 12 visual modules required by the governing specification.
- Storage: browser `localStorage` behind a typed persistence interface; no remote transmission.
- AI: no AI dependency in V1. Structured deterministic output is complete by itself.
- Status language: developmental research prototype, not validated, clinical, diagnostic, predictive, or suitable for high-stakes decisions.
- Proprietary boundary: a pinned, versioned port from `olsonan26/LOOKS-LIKE-COSTAR`; source content is hypothesis material and may be contradicted.
- Exclusions: prediction timelines, diagnosis, hiring, deception/violence prediction, medical inference, romantic destiny, and relationship directives.

## Definition of done

A user can start, consent, choose mode and goal, enter minimal context, answer an adaptive path, pause/resume, review, score, inspect accessible visual results, submit corrections, export, print/share selectively, and delete all local data. Results preserve all engine/content/proprietary versions and work with no network or AI service.

## World-class benchmark

V1 is judged internally on behavioral specificity, contextual expression, baseline-versus-stress separation, contradiction honesty, confidence transparency, visual usefulness, practical actions, dignity, accessibility, privacy, deterministic reproducibility, and evidence traceability. No public superiority claim is authorized.
