# Competitive Benchmark

This internal matrix compares category tendencies, not proprietary competitor content or current marketing claims.

| Category | Typical strength | V1 differentiation target |
|---|---|---|
| Coaching quizzes | Energetic action language | Evidence-linked actions and uncertainty |
| Big Five reports | Construct clarity | Context, contradiction, and relationship translation |
| DISC-style reports | Accessible work language | Baseline/stress separation and source transparency |
| Enneagram quizzes | Narrative recognition | Falsifiable behavioral evidence |
| Love-language tools | Simple relationship actions | Multi-construct needs, boundaries, and context |
| Strengths tools | Positive development framing | Friction conditions and confidence |
| Numerology apps | Proprietary symbolic depth | Hypotheses that may be contradicted |
| AI personality reports | Fluent synthesis | Reproducible measurement and complete offline fallback |

V1 is technically differentiated but cannot claim practical, measurement, or comparative superiority until pilot outcome studies support it.
