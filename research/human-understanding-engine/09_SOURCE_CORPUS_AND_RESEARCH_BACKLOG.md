# Source Corpus and Research Backlog

## Purpose

This file tells research agents what to study, why it matters, what evidence to seek, and what artifact to produce. Expert courses are inputs, not unquestioned authorities.

## Research priority order

### Priority 1 — Test construction and psychometrics

Study first:

- Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing
- International Personality Item Pool documentation
- Big Five and IPIP-NEO research
- HEXACO research
- item-writing guidance
- reliability and validity methods
- cognitive interviewing
- observer-report methodology
- measurement invariance
- accessible assessment design

Required outputs:

- practical psychometrics primer
- licensing table
- item-writing checklist
- validation ladder
- recommended pilot analyses

### Priority 2 — V1 constructs

Research the twelve V1 constructs directly. For each, locate:

- best-supported scientific definition
- established facets
- measurement instruments
- behavioral examples
- state and stress effects
- developmental considerations
- cultural limitations
- adjacent constructs and overlap
- practical relationship implications

### Priority 3 — Contradiction and contextual expression

Study:

- person-situation interaction
- self/observer agreement
- social desirability
- impression management
- compensatory behavior
- attachment and context
- stress-related personality expression
- developmental personality change
- narrative identity
- ecological momentary assessment where relevant

### Priority 4 — Recommendation science

Study evidence relevant to:

- feedback delivery
- implementation intentions
- emotion regulation
- conflict repair
- parenting communication
- autonomy-supportive communication
- motivational interviewing principles
- behavior change and habit formation

Recommendations should not exceed the evidence behind them.

## Research questions the project must answer

1. Does the combined engine outperform questionnaire-only interpretation?
2. Which chart hypotheses add incremental value?
3. Which chart hypotheses merely restate broad human tendencies?
4. Does observer context improve or distort accuracy?
5. Can baseline/stress separation predict real behavior better than one global score?
6. Which contradictions are stable and useful versus measurement noise?
7. Do practical recommendations improve communication or merely feel insightful?
8. Which graph forms improve understanding and which create false confidence?
9. How long can the quiz become before drop-off harms measurement?
10. Which constructs work similarly across self, child, partner, and general observer modes?
11. How does receiver-aware wording affect emotional impact without changing comprehension?
12. What evidence would cause the team to revise or remove a proprietary rule?

## Scope-control rule

No new course, theory, or construct enters V1 merely because it is fascinating.

To enter V1 it must:

- solve a defined user problem
- improve a report or recommendation
- be measurably distinct
- fit within length constraints
- have an ethical interpretation
- have an implementation and validation plan
